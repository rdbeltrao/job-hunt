# Fit profile: Your Name

Copy this file to `profile.md` (ignored by git) and replace every section with your own
facts. The scorer reads the whole file as plain text, so write it the way you would brief
a recruiter: concrete, factual, in English. The questions in `jobs/scoring/criteria.py`
refer to the sections below by name, so keep the headings.

## Target role

- Title range: Engineering Manager, Senior Engineering Manager, Head of Engineering. Team Lead or Tech Lead titles fit only when the role includes people management.
- Shape: player-coach, about 75% people management and 25% hands-on.
- Team: 6 to 10 engineers directly, or several teams through team leads.
- Product: customer-facing products. Name the domains you have worked in.
- Team type: say whether you prefer product teams or platform, infrastructure and SRE teams, and how strongly.
- Company stage: startup, scale-up, corporate; say which is a plus, a minus or neutral.
- Ways of working: name the practices you want (remote-first, asynchronous, continuous delivery, AI-assisted engineering) and the ones you avoid.

## Location and language

- Where you live and what you accept: remote (which countries), hybrid (how many office days a week are fine, how many are a minus), on-site, relocation.
- Languages you speak and at what level (for example: English fluent, Spanish B2, no German). Then say which language situations are a blocker (for example "a German-speaking team is a blocker even when the posting is in English") and which are only a minus (for example "a Spanish-speaking team is a minus").
- Work authorization: where you can work without sponsorship.

## Preferences confirmed on YYYY-MM-DD

Hard rules
- Employment type you accept (for example: employee contract only; contractor or freelance engagements are a blocker).

Minus, not a blocker
- Things you would rather avoid but would consider for an exceptional role.

- Things other people might mind that you do not (travel, title, company size).

## Deal-breakers (score low or skip)

- List each hard blocker on its own line: language, employment type, role level (individual contributor, VP/CTO), on-site outside your area, industries you refuse.

## Experience summary

- One bullet per role, newest first: company, title, dates, scope (teams, headcount), what you were responsible for, what you built or changed. Figures you are comfortable using for ranking.
- A line with your total years in software and years in leadership.

## Technical profile

How to weigh a posting's stack: say which technologies are core (many years, production), which you know well enough, which you only manage, and which are aspirations. The scorer treats a posting's required stack as heavy and a desired stack as light; tell it which of your skills count where.

- Core, many years hands-on and in production: ...
- Solid working knowledge: ...
- Managed, not hands-on: ...
- Aspirations only, no professional depth: ...

## Strengths beyond the stack

- What you have done repeatedly that postings ask for: building teams from scratch, scaling a platform, turning around delivery, insourcing from an agency, and so on.

## Management competencies

One paragraph listing the management work you have done: hiring, 1:1s, performance reviews, career frameworks, SDLC and agile practices, incident management, metrics, budgeting, stakeholder work.
